\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We presented relation-preserving modality transfer for open-vocabulary
predicate classification. Its central idea is to preserve source visual
relation geometry during triplet-semantic alignment while synthesizing visual
support for unseen predicates. Across Visual Genome and GQA, the method
consistently improves SDSGG, and ablations separate the benefit of VSP from the
base--novel trade-off introduced by novel synthesis. The present evidence is
limited to PredCls and one SDSGG-based implementation; extending the same
principles to detection-dependent OVSGG without assuming cross-baseline gains
is an important direction for future work.
