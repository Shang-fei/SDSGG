\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Language-guided OVSGG.}
Vision--language models enable relation recognition beyond supervised predicate
names. Epic learns entangled cross-modal prompts for unseen predicates
\cite{yu2023epic}, while RECODE decomposes relations into language-generated
subject, object, and spatial cues \cite{li2023recode}. RAHP introduces
entity- and region-aware hierarchical prompts \cite{liu2025rahp}; SDSGG uses
scene-specific LLM descriptions \cite{chen2024sdsgg}; and APT converts frozen
semantics into adaptive contextual prompts \cite{luo2026apt}. These methods
primarily improve the semantic targets used for matching. Our problem is
orthogonal: controlling the geometry of visual relation instances as they are
projected toward those targets.

\textbf{Structured transfer and synthesis.}
ACC models object interactions during knowledge infusion and end-to-end
transfer \cite{li2025acc}, addressing interaction mismatch rather than explicit
source-geometry preservation across visual-to-text projection. In zero-shot recognition, SHIP
learns synthesized prompts that reconstruct visual features and then samples
unseen-class features \cite{wang2023ship}. We adapt this idea to
subject--predicate--object semantics and couple it with visual structure
preservation, thereby addressing both missing novel support and distortion
during relation-level modality transfer.
