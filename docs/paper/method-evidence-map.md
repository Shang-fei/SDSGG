## Stable Manuscript Terminology

- **Relation-preserving modality transfer (RPMT):** the complete added branch.
- **Dominant-component removal (DCR):** removal of the leading shared direction from the triplet-text bank.
- **Visual structure preservation (VSP):** pairwise cosine-geometry preservation from visual-adapter features to projected features.
- **Novel synthesis (NS):** relation-conditioned generation of pseudo visual features for unseen predicates.
- **Triplet teacher:** frozen CLIP text embeddings conditioned jointly on subject, predicate, and object.

## Scope Restrictions

The manuscript must not assert completed SGCls or SGDet results, active text-structure or negative-alignment losses, active base replay or relationness, a low-rank projection interpretation of DCR, robustness before the reserved sweeps are measured, efficiency before common-hardware measurements are available, or portability to another baseline.
